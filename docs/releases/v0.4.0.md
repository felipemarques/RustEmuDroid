## Game controls

- **MOBA joystick** sends a touch from the game's movement stick toward a right-click destination. Place the hero marker on the character, match the joystick radius, and calibrate travel speed. A new click changes the destination. An optional stop binding lifts the contact early.
- **Free look** drags the camera with four direction bindings or a held mouse binding. **Scroll** turns mouse-wheel movement into a swipe from a fixed point. Both controls work in per-game schemes.
- **Single-touch wheel** is an option under Rotate. Place the control on the steering wheel, choose the starting angle and radius, then assign a key to steer each way. Existing two-finger rotation schemes keep their original gesture.

Open the editor with **Ctrl+Shift+A**. The Windows and Linux clients include the updated editor and the manuals show it in all three supported languages. [Controls and screenshots](https://github.com/felipemarques/RustEmuDroid/blob/v0.4.0/README.md)

## Performance and compatibility

SuperTuxKart 1.5 averaged 59.98 game FPS in two 0.4.0 Black Forest replays, compared to 60.01 on 0.3.0 with the same High Vulkan settings at the 60 FPS cap. Pooled P95 main-loop time was 18.90 ms on 0.4.0 and 19.12 ms on 0.3.0. [Replay conditions](https://github.com/felipemarques/RustEmuDroid/blob/v0.4.0/docs/game-testing.md#supertuxkart-release-comparison)

The free **Xtreme Rally URP** demo completed a timed start on the Sunset Dream stage on Android 17. On the RTX 5070 Ti, the game's surface presented 48.94 frames/s at the grid and 47.91 frames/s with the accelerator held. [Route, screenshot and raw measurements](https://github.com/felipemarques/RustEmuDroid/blob/v0.4.0/docs/game-testing.md#xtreme-rally-urp)

In a repeated PUBG MOBILE Inventory route, the Android 17 guest presented 25.04, 25.06 and 24.67 frames per second at Balanced, Smooth and Balanced Lobby graphics respectively. Each had a pause of at least one second. The independent Pie64 test reached the copied Android system and still stopped during vendor configuration. [Game measurements](https://github.com/felipemarques/RustEmuDroid/blob/v0.4.0/docs/game-testing.md) · [BlueStacks boot record](https://github.com/felipemarques/RustEmuDroid/blob/v0.4.0/docs/bluestacks-lab.md)
