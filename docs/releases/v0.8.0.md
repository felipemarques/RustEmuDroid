## Instant replay

Open the clock on the sidebar and enable the three-second replay buffer before a match. When a highlight happens, press **Ctrl+Shift+H** to save the preceding three seconds as an animated JPEG XL. The sidebar folder opens the saved file's directory. FFmpeg needs the `libjxl_anim` encoder.

Replay starts off in each Android session. The original **Ctrl+Shift+J** shortcut still captures the next three seconds. The in-app shortcut guide and the English, Brazilian Portuguese and Spanish manuals cover both actions.

In ANDAR at 1600 × 900 on an RTX 5070 Ti, the Android surface presented 31.12 and 32.00 frames/s with replay off, and 30.79 with replay on. The armed sample used about 24 MiB more desktop process memory.
