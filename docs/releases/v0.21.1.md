# RustEmuDroid v0.21.1

Move the pointer over the BlueStacks preview to scroll Android with the mouse wheel. Once you click the preview to activate the game controls, a mapped wheel direction sends its assigned action once. Unmapped directions still scroll Android.

Opening the controls menu or editor keeps wheel input in the RustEmuDroid window. After switching back to RustEmuDroid, move the pointer over the preview to scroll Android before clicking back into the game controls.
