## Switch control profiles during gameplay

Press **Ctrl+Shift+P** with a game open to activate its next saved control profile. The name appears over the game. With two profiles, such as *On foot* and *Driving*, each press switches between them. With more profiles, cycling past the last returns to the first.

Create layouts from the sidebar keyboard button under **Manage profiles**. The shortcut is also listed in **Help → Shortcuts**.
