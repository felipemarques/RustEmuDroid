## Lower CPU cost for the embedded display

The launcher can now upload a decoded Android frame directly to its OpenGL texture. The screenshot and recording controls retain access to the same frame. The direct path is selected on desktop OpenGL or OpenGL ES 3.0 and later. Older contexts keep the existing texture path.

On the Windows release build, the embedded player opened Level 1 of FPS WW2 URP. The sidebar camera button saved a 1600 × 900 PNG of the trench, including the game's own FPS counter.

In six Windows samples of the same FPS WW2 URP scene, mean frame preparation on the desktop fell from 1.22 to 0.21 ms. Launcher CPU use averaged 40.48% and 36.60% of one logical processor respectively. The Android game ran on the RTX 5070 Ti and Windows drew the desktop panel on the Radeon 780M. The test isolated a reduction in launcher work. Game presentation rates varied.
