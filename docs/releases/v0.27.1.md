# RustEmuDroid v0.27.1

On Windows, the content check of the engine's surface in the panel now also gives up a surface that shows nothing (one flat colour over the whole panel) while Android shows a picture, even when Android stands still, and it judges the picture only while the panel can really be seen. The audio output of v0.27.0, the engine, the Android images and existing profiles are unchanged.

**What was reported.** In the live tests of v0.27.0 the panel went back to copied frames four times in the first session, with `its picture on the screen stood still while Android's kept changing`, and showed the engine's surface again 60 seconds later each time, while Android kept animating. A false alarm of the check was the first suspect: something covering the client, the client on another monitor or partly off the screen, small changes such as the clock, or the two samples being taken at different moments.

**What was wrong.** The check was right. On the same test PC, with a disposable Android 17 profile and the stock engine 37.3.10, a capture of the image the computer sends to the monitor showed the engine's surface in the panel black from the moment the panel took it, while Android scrolled; the picture came back the moment the panel copied the frames again. The surface itself held nothing either. It was the same with v0.26.0, with the engine's graphics on either graphics card, and on both monitors. The engine started on its own, with its own window, showed Android on the same monitor at the same time. Four fallbacks are the most one session makes (the first and three retries), so every attempt had failed. On this PC the engine's surface had shown Android in the panel earlier the same day, in the v0.26.0 tests; in between, the monitors were rearranged (the monitor of the graphics card that runs the engine became the main one). That change is the only one found, but it is not proven to be the cause. Why the surface stays blank inside the panel there is not solved in this release.

Two weaknesses of the check came out of the same tests:

- **A blank surface over a still Android was never noticed.** The check only compared motion: with Home or any still page shown, the engine's frames did not change, so the panel stayed black for as long as Android stood still.
- **A panel partly off the desktop was still judged.** The points the check tests for a covering window were found inside the client's window even where no monitor shows them, so the part off the screen was read as black and counted as part of the picture.

**What changes.**

- **A blank screen counts against the surface.** A period in which the panel's area of the screen is one flat colour (every colour channel within 12 levels) while the engine's frame has a picture (some channel spans more than 48 levels), at least half of its pixels are more than 24 levels away from the screen's colour and so is its average colour by more than 16 levels, counts like a period in which the picture stood still. The dark Home of RustEmuDroid over a black panel is caught this way. Three such periods in a row, 1.5 seconds apart, and the panel copies the frames, with `its area of the screen stayed one flat colour while Android showed a picture` in **Settings → Launcher output** and **Technical details**, in the three languages. It is tried again 60 seconds later, three times at most, like a picture that stood still. An Android screen that is one colour itself (a black loading screen) counts for nothing, and so do a small light detail on a dark picture, which the screen's averaging may hide, and a fine pattern that averages to the screen's colour.
- **Both samples show nearly the same moment.** The panel's area of the screen is read when the engine's frame arrives, not before it is asked for.
- **What cannot be seen is no evidence.** As before, a period counts for nothing and the count starts again when something covers the middle or a corner of the panel, the client draws over more than a quarter of it, the window is minimised, the session is locked or the display is off. Now the same holds when one of those points is on no monitor, when the client's window is hidden, and when the system keeps the window out of sight (another virtual desktop).

**Where it was verified.** On the Windows test PC above (disposable Android 17 profile, stock engine 37.3.10, the engine drawing with the graphics card whose monitor is the main one), where the engine's surface stays blank in the panel, each scenario started while the panel showed the engine's surface, with Settings scrolling unless noted:

| Scenario | v0.27.0 check | v0.27.1 check |
|---|---|---|
| Home standing still, panel in view (25 s) | No fallback; the panel stayed black the whole time | Copied frames 4.6 seconds after the panel came into view (`one flat colour`) |
| Settings scrolling, panel in view (20 s) | Copied frames (`stood still`) | Copied frames (`one flat colour`) |
| An opaque window over the whole client (20 s) | Nothing judged while covered | Nothing judged while covered |
| Two thirds of the panel left of the desktop (20 s) | Judged: 3 strikes and copied frames while off the desktop | Nothing judged while off the desktop |
| Client minimised (20 s) | Nothing judged | Nothing judged |
| A click-through window over a corner of the panel, as a notification shows (4 times 8 s) | Copied frames | Copied frames |
| Client on the other monitor, driven by the other graphics card | Copied frames | Copied frames |
| Taps opening and closing Settings' search every 2 s, as in the v0.27.0 audio tests | Copied frames | Copied frames |

Every fallback in this table happened while the panel was in view and black on the monitor, so none was a false alarm; the panel showed Android again each time it copied the frames.

**Not verified:** a computer where the engine's surface shows Android in the panel, so the scenarios above could not count false alarms of a working surface; there the new rule only adds the blank case, and the rules for motion are as before. Why the surface stays blank on the test PC since the monitors were rearranged needs its own investigation. Until it is solved there, **Settings → Graphics → Android picture in the panel → Always copy the frames** avoids the few black seconds of each retry.

**Tests.** The offline tests run the check over sequences of samples: a screen that follows the engine, a still game, a screen that stands still while the engine moves, a blank screen over a still and over a moving Android, a black panel over a dark picture, an Android screen of one colour, a small light detail on a dark picture, a fine pattern that averages to one colour, noise and small changes such as the clock, periods in which the panel cannot be seen between strikes, and an engine or a screen without an answer. They also cover the reason text in the three languages, a hidden client window, and the retries after both reasons.
