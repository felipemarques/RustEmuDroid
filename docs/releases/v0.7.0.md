## Keyboard, mouse and gamepad controls

Open the keyboard icon on the sidebar while a game is running. Choose the keyboard/mouse or gamepad view to see its bindings over the game. Both devices remain available for play. Gamepad hints identify the analog stick and show familiar button names such as A / ×.

The quick menu now saves separate horizontal and vertical mouse sensitivity values for each game scheme. Drag or click the numbers to change them. The multipliers affect mouse aiming and mouse free look. The default is 1.00 on both axes for existing and new schemes.

In the controls editor, select a D-pad and choose the left stick, right stick, or neither. Its direction keys remain available alongside the selected stick. Previously saved D-pad mappings retain their behavior.
